## Story

**As a** platform engineer,
**I want** all required Kafka topics and consumer groups provisioned with appropriate retention and partition settings,
**So that** the event-driven components (sync, indexing, graph updates, state events) can communicate reliably and scalably.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Kafka is deployed in KRaft mode (no ZooKeeper) with 3 brokers as a StatefulSet
- [ ] The following topics are created with specified settings:

| Topic | Partitions | Retention | Consumers |
|---|---|---|---|
| `knowledge.source.synced` | 6 | 7 days | indexing-service |
| `knowledge.chunk.indexed` | 12 | 7 days | graph-agent |
| `knowledge.graph.updated` | 6 | 7 days | context-retrieval |
| `contextiq.state.events` | 12 | 24 hours | replay-service |
| `contextiq.governance.events` | 6 | 30 days | audit-logger |
| `contextiq.connector.health` | 3 | 1 hour | health-monitor |

- [ ] Consumer groups are pre-registered and validated at startup
- [ ] Kafka Connect is deployed for any future source/sink connector needs
- [ ] Broker metrics (lag, throughput, under-replicated partitions) are scraped by Prometheus
- [ ] KRaft cluster passes a chaos test: one broker failure results in < 5 s message delivery interruption
